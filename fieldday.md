GRARC and HVARC team together to celebrate Field Day

### WHERE

Grant County Fairgrounds<br>
Youth and Ag Building Kitchen and Midway field<br>
916 East Elm Street (County Road A)<br>
Lancaster, Wisconsin 53813

### When
27-28 June 2026<br>
1:00 PM on 27 June start
Clubs start setting up at 9:00 AM

### Details
The folks at Great River Amateur Radio Club have provided<span style="color:red">[details here](https://www.w0dbq.org/field-day-2026)</span>.
